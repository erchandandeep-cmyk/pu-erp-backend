REBRANDING + TEST ACCOUNTS - HOW TO MERGE
============================================
Two things in this patch:

1. Every page that said "ME-ERP" now says "PU ERP" (matches your
   mobile app's name). Also fixed a REAL bug (not just cosmetic): the
   application detail page, PDF export, and a legacy "department"
   field default were hardcoded to "Mechanical Engineering" for EVERY
   user regardless of their actual department - a Chemistry student's
   official record would have wrongly shown "Mechanical Engineering."
   Now pulls the real department dynamically. Tested.

2. seed_test_users now creates 7 accounts (all password TestPass123):
   test_student, test_teacher, test_hod (same department as each
   other), test_registrar, test_vc, test_dean_academic,
   test_dean_colleges. All tested logging in successfully with correct
   office assignments.
