# 45-Day ME-ERP Roadmap

## Week 1
Environment, Django basics, Git/GitHub, requirements, database/ER design.

## Week 2
Authentication, roles, profiles, admin.

## Week 3
Applications, attachments, workflow, approval/rejection/forwarding, history.

## Week 4
Announcements, notifications, document management.

## Week 5
Dashboards, analytics, search/filtering, audit logs, UI polish.

## Week 6
Testing, security review, PostgreSQL, deployment, report, PPT, viva.

## Final 3-4 days
Bug fixing, demonstration data, backup, documentation, deployment verification.
